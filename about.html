About Coinfish

We trade the process, not the hype.

Coinfish was built for people who want the markets to work for their life, not the other way around. No get-rich promises. No screenshots of one lucky trade. A repeatable way to put on defined-risk positions and the honesty to show what happens when they lose.

Why Coinfish exists

Most trading content sells a feeling. The big number, the lifestyle, the dopamine. It leaves out the part that actually builds wealth: a process you can run a thousand times, where the math is on your side and a single loss never takes you out.

We focus on credit spreads and iron condors on liquid underlyings like SPY, QQQ, and IWM. Defined risk on every trade. We get paid for being patient and structured, and we treat losing trades as part of the cost of doing business, not as a secret to hide.

What we believe

Money is a tool, not the goal

The point of trading well is freedom. Time with the people who matter, room to be generous, and something worth handing down. Profit is the means. Purpose is the reason.

Process beats prediction

Nobody knows what the market does tomorrow. We do not try to. We follow technicals for timing, the chain for structure, and scanners for selection, then we manage risk and let probability do its work over many trades.

Honesty compounds

Every recap shows the entry, the reasoning, and the result. When a trade goes wrong, we say so and explain what we would adjust. That is the only way anyone actually learns to trade.
