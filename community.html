Questions

Before you join

Do I need experience to join? +

No. The education starts from the ground up. If you have never placed an options trade, the Playbook and walkthroughs will get you to your first defined-risk position safely.

Is this signals or education? +

Both, but education comes first. We share what we are trading and why, so you learn to find and structure setups on your own rather than just copying alerts.

How much capital do I need? +

You can learn the process on a small account. Defined-risk spreads let you size positions to whatever you are comfortable risking. We never tell you to trade more than you can afford to lose.

Is any of this financial advice? +

No. Coinfish is educational. We show our process and our trades. Every decision in your own account is yours.
