How To Read This

What the numbers mean, and do not mean

A win rate without context is a vanity metric. Here is how we keep it honest.

Every position is sized to a defined maximum loss, so results are tracked in R multiples rather than dollars. A win rate near 70 percent is normal for credit strategies, because the losers are larger than the winners by design. The job is to keep the average loss controlled and the process repeatable.

This page is not a promise of returns. It is a record of what happened. Your results will differ based on your sizing, your timing, and your discipline. That is exactly why we teach the process instead of selling alerts.
